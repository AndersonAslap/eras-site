Who we are and what this policy covers

Eras is an entertainment app that uses artificial intelligence to write what your life would have been like in another time and place, and creates an image to illustrate the story.

This policy covers the Eras app, this website and the public pages of shared stories. Data controller: [COMPANY NAME AND ID — TO CONFIRM]. Data protection officer: contato@eras.app [TO CONFIRM].

Data you give us

We only ask for what the app needs to work:

  • Account: name, e-mail and password. The password is stored irreversibly (hashed) — not even we can read it.
  • Profile: language, theme and the default characteristics you choose to save (such as age and gender).
  • To generate a story: age, gender, era, place and realism level and, if you want, nickname, job and personality traits.
  • Ethnicity/ancestry: optional and handled as sensitive data (see its own section below).
  • Reports: the reason and text you send when reporting a shared story.

Data created when you use the app

  • The stories and images generated for you, with date, era and status.
  • How many stories you generated today, to apply the free daily limit.
  • Visits to the public links of your stories, counted without storing the visitor’s IP address.
  • Technical security logs, without e-mail, password, tokens or ethnicity.
  • App crash reports (Sentry), when enabled: device model, OS and app version and the error, without personally identifiable data.

What the app does not collect

Eras does not collect your location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising ID or other apps’ history. The app shows no ads and does not sell or rent personal data.

Device permissions

The app only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it, and you can deny or revoke it in your device settings:

  • Photos/gallery: only to save the story card to your gallery when you tap “Save”. The app does not read your photos.
  • Notifications: local reminders scheduled on the device itself (for example, when your daily quota renews). We send no marketing notifications.
  • Biometrics (Face ID or fingerprint): optional, to open the app without typing your password. The check is done by the device’s system; Eras never receives your biometrics.

Data stored on your device

Access tokens are kept in the system’s secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Preferences such as theme and language stay in the app’s local storage. All of it is erased when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • Creating and keeping your account, generating, storing and sharing your stories — performance of contract (the terms of use).
  • Ethnicity/ancestry to write the story — specific, highlighted consent.
  • Daily limit, content moderation, abuse prevention and account security — legitimate interest.
  • Account verification and password recovery e-mails — performance of contract. We send no marketing e-mails without your permission.
  • Keeping access logs for the period required by law — legal obligation (Brazil’s Marco Civil da Internet).

Sensitive personal data: ethnicity/ancestry

It is optional and only processed with your explicit consent, for the sole purpose of writing the story. It never appears on the story’s public page, never goes into technical logs and is deleted with the story or your account. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app’s Profile.

Artificial intelligence and other providers

To write and illustrate stories, we send only the story data — never your e-mail or full name — to AI providers such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, fal.ai or Replicate [TO CONFIRM active providers]. Texts also go through automated moderation. Under these providers’ contracts, data sent through the API is not used to train their models [TO CONFIRM per provider].

We also use hosting, database, image storage, transactional e-mail and crash monitoring (Sentry) providers [TO CONFIRM list], which process data only on our behalf and under our instructions.

Some of these providers are outside Brazil. In those cases, the international transfer follows article 33 of the LGPD, with contractual clauses and adequate safeguards.

Shared stories

When you create a link, the public page shows the title, the story, the image, the era and, if you allow it, your nickname. Anyone with the link can see it. You can disable the link or delete the story at any time — the link goes offline immediately.

How long we keep data

  • Account and stories: for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted story: the link goes offline and the image and ethnicity are erased immediately; the text stays hidden for up to 30 days to handle reports and is then removed [TO CONFIRM period].
  • Stories that failed to generate: erased after 30 days [TO CONFIRM period].
  • Sessions: access expires after 30 days without use.
  • Deleted account: all data, stories, images and links are permanently erased; we keep only what the law requires (such as access logs, for 6 months).

How we protect your data

All communication uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored with strong hashing, session tokens are rotating and stored encrypted, access to data is restricted per person and every query is scoped to the user’s own data. No system is 100% secure: if a relevant incident happens, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

Your rights and how to delete your account

Under the LGPD, you can confirm whether we process your data, access, correct, port or delete it, learn who we share it with and withdraw consent.

To delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account → “Delete my account forever”. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you cannot access the app, request deletion by e-mail to contato@eras.app [TO CONFIRM] from your account’s e-mail; we reply within 15 days.

Children and teenagers

Eras is meant for people aged 16 or over [TO CONFIRM], and the minimum age is confirmed at sign-up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, it will be deleted.

This website

The Eras website uses no cookies and asks for no personal data. When enabled, visit statistics use Plausible, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data — that’s why there is no cookie banner.

Changes to this policy and contact

If this policy changes in a relevant way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Questions, requests or complaints: contato@eras.app [TO CONFIRM]. You can also contact Brazil’s data protection authority (ANPD).
