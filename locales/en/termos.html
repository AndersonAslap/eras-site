The service

Eras generates fictional stories inspired by historical context, with images created by artificial intelligence, for entertainment. It is not official educational content or genealogical research.

Account and usage limit

You need an account to generate stories. Free use has a daily limit that may change to keep the service sustainable.

Acceptable use

Don’t use Eras to create sexual, violent, discriminatory or offensive content, or to impersonate anyone. Fields with offensive terms are rejected, and reported stories may be hidden.

AI-generated content

Stories and images are generated automatically and may contain historical inaccuracies. You may freely share your stories for personal use [TO CONFIRM license].

Public links

Shared links can be seen by anyone who has them. You can disable them or delete the story at any time.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will let you know in the app. Questions: contato@eras.app [TO CONFIRM]. Jurisdiction: [TO CONFIRM].
