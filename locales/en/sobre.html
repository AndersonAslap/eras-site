Where Eras came from

An upgrade of social media quizzes: instead of half a dozen ready-made answers, a whole life written for you.

The idea

Everyone has taken a “which character would you be?” quiz. It was fun, but the results were pre-programmed: half a dozen answers for millions of people.

On Eras, the answer is written on the spot by an artificial intelligence, based on who you are and the era and place you choose. The results are practically endless.

How the AI uses history

Each catalog era has curated context: key events, how society was organized, common jobs, life expectancy and curiosities. That context travels with your request to the AI.

In realistic mode, the story follows what was likely for someone like you in that place. In fantastic mode, fate gets more creative, but the era stays recognizable. Either way, you get the real facts that inspired the story.

The tone: always light — and respectful

The stories are funny even when the ending is tragic — and let’s be honest, in history almost every ending was. But the humor never targets the suffering of oppressed groups: slavery, persecution and violence are treated with respect, without graphic descriptions and without stereotypes.

Limits worth knowing

The story is fiction inspired by real history; it is not a lesson or genealogical research. The AI can get details wrong, which is why we keep the “real facts” separate. If something looks wrong or offensive, report it from the story’s link.

Privacy

We only ask for what’s needed to write your story. Ethnicity is optional, requires consent and never shows on public links. You can delete stories or your whole account anytime.
